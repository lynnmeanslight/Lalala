## The Project: Lalala

**The problem:** Thailand's e-commerce market is dominated by Lazada and Shopee. Sellers pay 3–5% in opaque fees, get suspended without appeal, and wait days for payment. Buyers are surrounded by fake reviews and have no real protection when orders go wrong.

**The solution:** Lalala is a Thai marketplace where every transaction is protected by a USDT escrow smart contract on KUB Chain. The buyer's USDT is locked on-chain at checkout and only released to the seller after confirmed delivery. Fake reviews are structurally impossible because you cannot post one unless the smart contract confirms you completed a real purchase. The platform fee is 1.5%, fixed, and enforced by the contract not a terms-of-service promise.

**Why it fits the hackathon theme:** Lalala solves a real, everyday problem for millions of Thai sellers and buyers right now. It is not a concept it is a working product with a clear revenue model, a defined user base, and infrastructure built on KUB Chain, which already has millions of active Thai users through Bitkub.

**Why it wins on the judging criteria:**

| Criterion | How Lalala Answers It |
|---|---|
| Originality (30%) | First Thai marketplace with on-chain escrow and structurally verified reviews not a feature on top of an existing platform, but a new platform where trust is built into the contract |
| Problem-Solving (30%) | Directly addresses the fee, fake review, and payment protection problems that dominate Thai seller communities daily |
| Completeness (20%) | Core flow list, pay in USDT, escrow locks, delivery confirms, funds release is fully functional and demonstrable in the pitch |
| Scalability (20%) | Thailand first, then Vietnam, Indonesia, and the Philippines same contracts, same model, new market each time |
